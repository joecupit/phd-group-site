## Abstract summary

An experimentally validated algal model links illumination and nutrient conditions to biomass and lipid production. Chlorophyll fluorescence is incorporated as an observable indicator, supporting future monitoring and evaluation of operating conditions for biodiesel-oriented cultivation.
