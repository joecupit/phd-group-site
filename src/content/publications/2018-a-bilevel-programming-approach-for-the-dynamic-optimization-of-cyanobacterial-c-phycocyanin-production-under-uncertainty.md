## Abstract summary

A robust bilevel formulation optimises cyanobacterial pigment production while considering uncertainty in kinetic parameters. Light and nutrient decisions are evaluated against adverse parameter combinations to estimate achievable production ranges and reduce over-optimistic expectations.
