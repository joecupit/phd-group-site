## Abstract summary

A graph-network surrogate incorporates nozzle shape into predictions of multiphase flow. Models trained on adaptive-mesh fluid simulations use geometry embeddings and additional graph connections to improve sensitivity to design changes and support faster evaluation of unseen configurations.
