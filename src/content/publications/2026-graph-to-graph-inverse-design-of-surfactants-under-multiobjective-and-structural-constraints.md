## Abstract summary

A motif-based graph-to-graph framework designs surfactant molecules by optimising latent representations for multiple target properties while enforcing structural constraints. The targets are critical micelle concentration, air–water surface tension at that concentration, and maximum surface excess concentration. Constraints account for surfactant classes and compatibility between amphiphiles and counterions. Compared with existing surfactant-property models and the authors’ earlier SMILES-based transformer, the framework improves property prediction and the validity of inverse-design outputs, while generating a wider range of surfactant classes.
