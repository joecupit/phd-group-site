## Abstract summary

MolPrice estimates molecular prices as an economically interpretable indicator of synthetic accessibility. Self-supervised training and comparisons of molecular representations examine generalisation to unfamiliar compounds, connecting molecular design with practical purchasing and synthesis considerations.
