## Abstract summary

Removing inactive constraints helps maintain tractability in cutting-set methods for robust optimisation. A heat-pump incentive-allocation problem illustrates the approach under uncertain prices and climate conditions, connecting computational methods with economic, environmental and distributional outcomes.
