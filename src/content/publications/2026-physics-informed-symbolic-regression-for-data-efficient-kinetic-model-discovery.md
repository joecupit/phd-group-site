## Abstract summary

Physics-informed symbolic regression constrains kinetic-model discovery using prior mechanistic knowledge. Parameter uncertainty is sampled with Metropolis–Hastings to provide prediction intervals, and catalytic case studies assess the resulting gains in interpretability and data efficiency.
