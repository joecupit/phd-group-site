## Abstract summary

Conceptual process design and life-cycle assessment compare citrus-waste and algal feedstocks for polylimonene carbonate. The analysis identifies environmental burdens across oxidation, polymer synthesis and energy use, showing why renewable feedstocks alone do not ensure a sustainable process.
