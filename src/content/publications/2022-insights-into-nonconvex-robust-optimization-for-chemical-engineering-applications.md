## Abstract summary

Several methods for nonconvex semi-infinite optimisation are compared across chemical-engineering benchmarks. The study distinguishes approaches that restrict or relax feasible regions and assesses their ability to obtain dependable solutions when uncertain nonlinear constraints cannot be reformulated directly.
