## Abstract summary

Experiments with engineered Chlamydomonas reinhardtii investigate how temperature and illumination affect growth and bisabolene synthesis. Kinetic modelling separates growth-associated behaviour from product formation and helps identify conditions appropriate to biofuel-precursor production.
