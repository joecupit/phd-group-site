## Abstract summary

This edited book develops modelling methods for chemical and biochemical reaction engineering. Chapters cover mechanistic, data-driven and hybrid models, parameter estimation, uncertainty, reaction-network reduction and transfer of knowledge between processes, with worked applications illustrating their use.
