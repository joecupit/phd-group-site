## Abstract summary

A geometry-conditioned graph neural network approximates spray simulations for different nozzle shapes. Shape embeddings, boundary-to-volume conditioning and attention mechanisms are investigated to capture geometric influences on flow while reducing the computational cost of repeated fluid-dynamics calculations.
