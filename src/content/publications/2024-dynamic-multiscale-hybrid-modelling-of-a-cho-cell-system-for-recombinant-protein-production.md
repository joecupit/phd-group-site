## Abstract summary

A multiscale model couples enzyme-constrained metabolic flux analysis with extracellular hybrid kinetics for CHO-cell protein production. It connects medium and feeding changes to intracellular behaviour, supporting interpretation and predictive simulation of fed-batch cultures.
