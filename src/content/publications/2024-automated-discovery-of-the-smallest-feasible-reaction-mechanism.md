## Abstract summary

SiMBA searches for compact reaction mechanisms consistent with kinetic observations. Candidate mechanisms are translated into dynamic models, fitted and compared, providing an automated starting point for mechanistic investigation while leaving chemical identification of intermediates to expert interpretation.
