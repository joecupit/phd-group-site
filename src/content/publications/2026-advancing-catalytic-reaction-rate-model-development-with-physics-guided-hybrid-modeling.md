## Abstract summary

A hybrid catalytic model links power-law parameters to surface intermediates and incorporates thermodynamic and mechanistic constraints. Methanol synthesis and water–gas shift measurements demonstrate how physics-guided training can improve predictions and support interpretation of catalyst behaviour.
