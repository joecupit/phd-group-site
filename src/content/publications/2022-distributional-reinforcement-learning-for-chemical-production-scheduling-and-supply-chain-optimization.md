## Abstract summary

A zero-order reinforcement-learning method addresses uncertain production schedules and inventory decisions across supply chains. It accommodates scheduling restrictions and risk-sensitive objectives, with comparisons against mathematical programming examining expected performance, adverse outcomes and computational efficiency.
