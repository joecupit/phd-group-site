## Abstract summary

A feeding strategy combines dissolved-oxygen-based glucose control with optimised anthranilic acid addition for engineered Escherichia coli. Kinetic modelling supports analysis and scale-up of tryptophan production while addressing inefficient substrate conversion and acetate formation.
