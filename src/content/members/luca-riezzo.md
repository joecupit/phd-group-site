Luca Riezzo is a PhD researcher in the “Intelligent Reaction and Process Systems Engineering Research Group”. After graduating with an MEng in Chemical Engineering in 2025, he began doctoral research focused on applying machine learning within chemical engineering to improve process understanding, modelling, and optimisation. His PhD project, “An AI-Enabled Self-Driven Laboratory Framework for Catalytic Reaction Kinetics Discovery,” combines physics-guided symbolic regression with model-based design of experiments to accelerate the development of interpretable kinetic models for catalytic reaction systems. His research aims to reduce experimental burden, improve model transparency, and support the development of self-driving laboratories, digital twins, and more efficient chemical manufacturing.
