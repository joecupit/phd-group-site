## The challenge

Bioprocesses connect biological activity with the physical environment of a reactor.
Cell metabolism, nutrient availability, mixing and light exposure can all influence
production. These interactions make it difficult to predict how a process will respond
to new conditions or behave at a different scale.

We develop models that connect biological and engineering descriptions of these systems.
Our work spans cell culture, fermentation and illuminated bioreactors, with the aim of
supporting process understanding, design and scale-up.

## Our approach

### Linking cell metabolism with reactor behaviour

Our multiscale cell-culture framework links reactor-scale dynamics with intracellular
metabolic fluxes. It combines a hybrid kinetic model with enzyme-constrained dynamic
metabolic flux analysis and uses bootstrapping to estimate uncertainty. A published case
study considers antibody production by Chinese Hamster Ovary cells, relating changing
culture conditions to predicted metabolic activity.

### Resolving mixing and light distribution

At the reactor scale, we couple computational fluid dynamics with reaction and transport
models. Work on miniature stirred photobioreactors examines how mixing and light
distribution interact with biological growth. This supports analysis of operating
conditions while recognising the spatial variation that a well-mixed model may overlook.

### Predicting performance across scales

For scale-up, we investigate how hybrid models developed with limited laboratory data
can be adapted using pilot-scale information. A yeast-fermentation study tests the
resulting predictions at industrial scale and examines how the balance of mechanistic
and data-driven detail affects model transfer.

### Investigating algae-based carbon capture

We also use biokinetic modelling to investigate algae-based carbon capture. Experimental
work with Dunaliella tertiolecta examines how light and temperature influence growth and
carbon conversion in a bicarbonate/carbonate medium. This connects biological process
understanding with a renewable application.
